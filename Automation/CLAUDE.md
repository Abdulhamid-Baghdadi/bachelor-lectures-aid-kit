# Automation Lab

Interactive labs for two TU Wien courses, Automatisierung and Modellbildung: show the notes' own models and equation
numbers, not simplified versions. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Sources: Kugi (2015), `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\Automatisierung\AutomatisierungVO.pdf`,
and Kugi et al. (2015), `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\ModellBildung\Modellbildung_Kapitel_*.pdf`
(page numbers are the printed "Seite N"). UI in English; the German term in brackets where it helps.
Published: GitHub Pages from main of Abdulhamid-Baghdadi/automation-lab. Companion of electrodynamics-lab and circuit-lab.
