# Electrodynamics Lab

Interactive labs for the key equations of electrodynamics, for advanced students: show the full equation sets, not a
simplified version. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Source of every equation: Prechtl (2010), `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\Elektrodynamik\Elektrodynamik_Vorlesung_v2010_ONLINE_unlocked.pdf`
(PDF page = printed page + 5). Notation as in the notes: γ conductivity, ϱ charge density, Ψ electric flux.

Einstein (1905), `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\Relativitätstheorie\Einstein1905\1905_17_891-921.pdf`
