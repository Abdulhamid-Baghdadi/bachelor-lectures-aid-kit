Electrodynamics Lab

Move the sliders and watch the key equations of electrodynamics and wave propagation work. After the TU Wien lecture notes of Prechtl (2010), Vorlesungen über Elektrodynamik, and Lasser (2012), Wellenausbreitung.

A learning aid: ideal materials, typical material values, numerical integrals on coarse grids. Equation numbers refer to the lecture notes; no text of them is reproduced. Companion to the Circuit Lab. Source.
