Mathematics Lab

After Taschner's script for Mathematik 1 und 2 for electrical engineering, TU Wien.

A learning aid for TU Wien's Mathematik 1 and 2: every equation comes from Taschner's script, by printed page; no text of it is reproduced. Linear differential equations and the Laplace transform are in the Automation Lab, Fourier series in the Signals and Systems Lab, vector analysis in the Vector Calculus Lab.
