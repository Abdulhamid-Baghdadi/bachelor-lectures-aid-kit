# Measurement

Interactive labs for TU Wien's Messtechnik (376.045) and Messtechnik-Labor (376.046): one lab per idea, from repeated
readings to quantisation noise. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Sources, in `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\`: the course book Schrüfer et al. (2012),
*Elektrische Messtechnik*, 10th edition, split into chapter PDFs in `Messtechnik\Buch\` (text layer; printed page =
chapter start + PDF page − 1, the starts 17, 83, 195, 248, 280, 304, 337, 377, 415 from the contents; umlauts partly
garbled), the lecture slides of 2016 in `Messtechnik\VO\`, and the lab script `Messtechnik-labor\MT-Labor_376046_2015_Skriptum.pdf`
by Schitter (2015) (printed Seite = PDF page − 4). The lab script has tasks but no solutions; practice comes from the
book's Beispiele and tables or is marked worked by hand. Spectrum analysis belongs to the Signals and Systems lab,
three-phase power to the Power engineering lab, Bode plots to the Automation lab, PCM to the Telecommunications lab.
Check every equation against the book and cite equation number and page. Part of `../PLAN.md`; not published yet.
