Measurement

After the TU Wien course book of Schrüfer et al. (2012), Elektrische Messtechnik, and the lab script of Schitter (2015).

A learning aid: ideal sources unless a lab says otherwise, readings drawn from seeded generators so every visit looks the same. Equation numbers and pages refer to the book's 10th edition; no text of it is reproduced. Spectrum analysis, the DFT and windows are in the Signals and Systems lab; three-phase power in the Power engineering lab; Bode plots in the Automation lab; PCM in the Telecommunications lab.
