Power engineering

Move the sliders and watch three phases share their power, lines and transformers sag and swell, faults strike and generators hold or slip, and fields turn the machines. After the TU Wien lecture notes of Gawlik (2015), Energieversorgung, and Schrödl (2012), Elektrische Maschinen und Antriebe.

A learning aid: symmetric systems, steady states, lumped models and per-unit example values where a lab says so. Equation numbers and pages refer to the lecture notes; no text of them is reproduced. Phasor arithmetic, Bode plots and control loops are in the Automation lab; harmonics and distortion power in the Signals and Systems lab; waves on lines in the Electrodynamics lab.
