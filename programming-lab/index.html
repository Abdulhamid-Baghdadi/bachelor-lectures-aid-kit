Programming Lab

After the course book of Klima and Selberherr (2010), Programmieren in C, and the language documentation named in each lab.

A learning aid for TU Wien's Programmieren 1 and 2 (2015): every algorithm follows the book, by section and page; no text of it is reproduced. The memory models are simplified to the rules their sources state; real runtimes do much more.
