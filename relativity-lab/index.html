Special Relativity Lab

Drag the events and move the sliders: frames, clocks, rods and fields seen from two frames at once. After Einstein (1905), Zur Elektrodynamik bewegter Körper.

A learning aid for TU Wien's Relativitätstheorie course, which left no script: every equation comes from Einstein (1905), by section and page; no text of it is reproduced. Maxwell's equations and the fields of resting charges and currents are in the Electrodynamics Lab.
