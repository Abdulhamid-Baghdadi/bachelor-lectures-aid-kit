# Semiconductors Lab

Interactive labs for TU Wien's Halbleiterphysik and Elektronische Bauelemente, fused: one lab per idea, from bands to the
MOSFET. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Sources: Strasser (2015), `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\Halbleiterphysik\HL_skript_2015_2.pdf`
(has a text layer; printed page = PDF page); exercises in `Halbleiterphysik\UE\` (sheets 1, 2, 10 as text, solutions
scanned). Hobler (2009), `Elektronische Bauelemente\Aufgabensammlung\bee2009.pdf`; its worked solutions
`ElektronischeBauelementeVU_Lösungswege.pdf` and the `Merkblätter` are scans without text.
Check every equation and constant against the script before using it; cite section, page and equation number.
Beginner circuits belong to the circuit lab; start above that level. Part of `../PLAN.md`; not published yet.
