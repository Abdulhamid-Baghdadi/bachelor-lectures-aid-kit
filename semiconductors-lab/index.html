Semiconductors, devices and circuits

Move the sliders and watch the bands, the carriers, the devices and the circuits respond. After the TU Wien lecture notes of Strasser (2015), Halbleiterphysik, the exercise collection of Hobler (2009), Elektronische Bauelemente, and the lecture notes of Zimmermann (2010), Schaltungstechnik.

A learning aid: silicon at 300 K unless a lab says otherwise, all dopants ionised, the depletion approximation for junctions. Sections, pages and equation numbers refer to the three texts; no text of them is reproduced. Circuits at beginner level are in the circuit lab; Bode and Nyquist diagrams are in the Automation lab, signals and filters in the Signals and Systems lab.
