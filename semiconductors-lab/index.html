Semiconductors Lab

Move the sliders and watch the bands, the carriers and the devices respond. After the TU Wien lecture notes of Strasser (2015), Halbleiterphysik, and the exercise collection of Hobler (2009), Elektronische Bauelemente.

A learning aid: silicon at 300 K unless a lab says otherwise, all dopants ionised, the depletion approximation for junctions. Sections, pages and equation numbers refer to the two texts; no text of them is reproduced. Circuits with diodes and transistors at beginner level are in the circuit lab; the Signals and Automation labs hold the systems around them.
