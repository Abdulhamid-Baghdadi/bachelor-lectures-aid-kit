# Signals and Systems Lab

Interactive labs for TU Wien's Signals and systems 1 (continuous) and 2 (discrete), fused: one lab per idea, with a
"Continuous | Discrete" switch where both exist. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Sources: Prechtl (2010), `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\SS1\Signale_und_Systeme_1_Skriptum_2010_ONLINE.unlocked.pdf`;
Doblinger (2015), `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\SS2\Doblinger_SuS_3Ed_unlocked.pdf`, with its
MATLAB/Octave examples beside it. Exercises: SS1 `Signale_und_Systeme1_Aufgabensammlung_v2009.pdf`, SS2 `sus2ue.pdf`.
Leave Laplace, z-transform, Bode, Nyquist and state space to the Automation Lab. Part of the one repository planned in
`../PLAN.md`; not published yet.
