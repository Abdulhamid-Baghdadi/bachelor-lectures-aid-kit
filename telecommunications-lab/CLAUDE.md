# Telecommunications

Interactive labs for TU Wien's Telekommunikation (389.138): one lab per idea, from PCM to the receiver's noise figure.
Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests: `node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Sources: Goiser (2016), the lecture slides in `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\Telekommunikation Hamid\VO\`
(text layers; printed slide number = PDF page; formulas partly garbled in the text, so check a reading against a seeded
simulation), and the course formulary `H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\Telekommunikation Hamid\formulary.pdf` (dated 2017-03-07, no author). No exercises or exams with
worked solutions are in that folder: practice comes from the slides' worked examples or is marked worked by hand.
AM and FM are in none of the 2016 sets. Sampling, Fourier and filters belong to the Signals and Systems lab;
free-space link budgets, fading and delay spread to the Electrodynamics lab. Check every equation against the slides
and cite set and slide. Part of `../PLAN.md`; not published yet.
