Telecommunications

Move the sliders and watch bits become pulses, pulses meet noise, and codes and receivers fight back. After the TU Wien lecture slides of Goiser (2016), Telekommunikation, and the course's formulary.

A learning aid: white Gaussian noise, ideal synchronisation unless a lab says otherwise, noise drawn from seeded generators so every visit looks the same. Slide numbers refer to the lecture sets; no text of them is reproduced. Sampling, the Fourier transform and filters are in the Signals and Systems lab; free-space link budgets, fading and delay spread in the Electrodynamics lab.
