# Vector Calculus Lab

Interactive labs for TU Wien's Mathematik 3 for electrical engineering (Taschner), the vector-analysis part: line
integrals, Green, Stokes and Gauss, one lab per idea. Layers and rules: ARCHITECTURE.md. Tests:
`node --test "tests/unit/*.test.js"`.
Taschner's own text, Anwendungsorientierte Mathematik, Band 3 (Hanser), was not available. Source of every equation:
Fritzsche (2003), Mathematik 3 für Elektrotechnik und IT, Wuppertal, in
`H:\My Drive\02_Personal\02_Education\TU\01_TU_Bachelor\Mathematik 3\Fritzsche\` (`metkap9.pdf`, `metkap10.pdf`; the
formulas are lost in the text layer: render the pages). Printed page = PDF page for chapter 9, PDF page + 57 for
chapter 10. Fritzsche numbers no equations: cite by section and page. The exam questions (`M3_Fragenkatalog.pdf`) also
ask for differential forms, not covered yet. Maxwell's equations stay in the Electrodynamics Lab; link to it.
Part of `../PLAN.md`.
