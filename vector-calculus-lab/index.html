Vector Calculus Lab

After the lecture notes of Fritzsche (2003), Mathematik 3 für Elektrotechnik und IT, Bergische Universität Wuppertal, chapters 9 and 10.

A learning aid for TU Wien's Mathematik 3 for electrical engineering. Taschner's own text for the course was not at hand, so every equation comes from Fritzsche (2003), by section and page; no text of it is reproduced. Integrals are sums on fixed grids, so both sides of each theorem are computed apart. Maxwell's equations, where these theorems do their work, are in the Electrodynamics Lab.
